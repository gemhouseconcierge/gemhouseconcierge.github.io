Diamond weight estimator

Estimate carat weight from millimetre measurements using standard trade formulas.

Estimated weight: –

Formulas (length × width × depth × factor): round – average diameter² × depth × 0.0061; oval 0.0062; pear 0.0059; marquise 0.0058; emerald 0.0080–0.0092 by length-to-width ratio; princess 0.0083; cushion 0.0081; heart 0.0059. A girdle correction is applied for thicker girdles. Results are estimates, typically within a few per cent for well-cut stones.
