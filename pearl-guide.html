Pearl grading guide

An interactive guide to the seven value factors for natural and cultured pearls.

Based on the seven pearl value factors used in the trade. Tick the grade that best fits your pearl or strand to build a summary.

Size

Measured in millimetres across the diameter (round) or length × width. Size affects rarity – especially for saltwater pearls.

Shape

From most to least symmetrical.

Colour

Body colour plus any overtone (a translucent colour floating over the surface) and orient (iridescence).

Lustre

The intensity and sharpness of light reflected from the surface – often the most important value factor.

Surface

The number, size and visibility of blemishes such as spots, bumps, wrinkles, chips and pits.

Nacre quality

Thickness and condition of the nacre layers (cultured pearls).

Matching

For strands and pairs – how uniform the pearls are in all factors.
